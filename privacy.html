EnergyBe

Privacy Policy · Last updated May 2026

No tracking · No ads · No backend

What EnergyBe is

EnergyBe is a free, non-commercial iOS app that lets Belgian households visualise their electricity and gas consumption from their Fluvius smart meter. It was built as a personal project with no business model, no subscriptions, and no advertising. All data stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Fluvius credentials — your email address and password are stored in the iOS Keychain. They are transmitted only to login.fluvius.be over HTTPS and never to any developer-controlled server.
  • Energy consumption data — meter readings fetched from the Fluvius API are cached on your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • App preferences — tariff rates, biometric lock setting, and onboarding state are stored in UserDefaults on your device only.

What EnergyBe does not do

  • Does not send any personal data to the developer or any third party
  • Does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • Does not have a backend server or database
  • Does not share your data with anyone

Third-party services the app contacts

  • Fluvius (fluvius.be) — to authenticate and fetch your meter data. Governed by Fluvius's own privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — to fetch historical outdoor temperatures for the temperature correlation feature. Open-Meteo is a free open-source weather API; no account or personal data is required or sent.

How to delete all your data

Open EnergyBe → Profile → Delete Account & Data. This permanently removes all credentials, cached energy data, and preferences from your device immediately. Nothing is stored elsewhere to delete.

Children

EnergyBe is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will be updated. Because EnergyBe has no backend, you will not receive a push notification — check this page when updating the app.
